How we validate

A wrong verdict on a customer report is the most expensive mistake a quality tool can make. So every analysis in Mensuro has to reproduce published reference results before it ships.

The rule: each analysis is checked against worked examples from the standards and textbooks quality engineers already trust (AIAG MSA 4th edition, Bosch Booklet No. 10, Montgomery's Introduction to Statistical Quality Control and the NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook), to the precision those sources print. The checks run automatically on every change to the engine. Statistics come from proven libraries (SciPy and statsmodels); Mensuro adds only the domain formulas, and those are what the checks cover.

What this does and doesn't mean

Matching published examples shows the formulas, constants and decision rules are implemented correctly. It doesn't make a study valid on its own: the data still has to come from a sound study (the right parts, operators and readings), and your customer's own requirements apply where they differ. Every Mensuro result and report shows its receipt (method, constants, sample size and settings) so anyone can check how a number was made.
