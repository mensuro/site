Validation

How we check the math.

A wrong verdict on a customer report is the one mistake that matters. These rules apply to every analysis we ship.

Reference results first

Before an analysis ships, it must reproduce published reference results to several decimal places, and those tests rerun on every change.

Published methods

Gage R&R follows AIAG MSA 4th edition and Type 1 studies follow Bosch Booklet No. 10. Each report names the method, the constants and the decision rules it used.

A receipt on every result

Method, alpha, sample size and corrections appear with every result, so an SQE can trace any number back to how it was made.

Your data, deleted by default

Uploads are used to compute your report and then deleted, unless you choose to save the study to your library.

Questions

Before you join

Is my measurement data safe?

Uploads are used only to compute your report and are deleted afterward unless you save the study. Sigmi sees column names, summary statistics and results, not your raw measurements. We never sell your data, and we share it only with the services that run Mensuro, listed on our privacy page.

Which methods and standards does it follow?

Gage R&R follows AIAG MSA 4th edition with both the ANOVA and Average & Range methods. Type 1 studies use the Cg and Cgk method of Bosch Booklet No. 10. Capability uses the standard Cp, Cpk, Pp and Ppk definitions with a normality check.

Does it work on a Mac or a locked-down company laptop?

Yes. Mensuro runs in any modern browser, so there is nothing to install and nothing for IT to approve on your machine.

Can I put my company's logo on reports?

Yes, on Pro, Team and founding memberships. The customer-facing report leads with your brand; Mensuro appears only in a small footer line.

When does the beta open?

Early 2027. Everyone on the early-access list hears first, in the order they joined.

Is the founding price refundable?

Yes. If we don't ship, you get a full refund. Joining the early-access list itself is free.
