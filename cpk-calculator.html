    How it's calculated

    The formulas, and where they come from.

    Standard definitions, nothing proprietary. Every number above comes from these steps.

    Overall: Pp and Ppk

    Overall sigma is the sample standard deviation of all readings, s = √[Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)].

    Pp = (USL − LSL) / 6s

    Ppk = min(USL − x̄, x̄ − LSL) / 3s

    Within: Cp and Cpk

    Within sigma measures short-term variation. For individual readings it is the average moving range divided by d2 = 1.128. For subgroups it is the average subgroup range divided by d2 for that subgroup size.

    σwithin = MR̄ / 1.128 or R̄ / d2

    Cp and Cpk use the Pp and Ppk formulas with σwithin in place of s.

    Expected and observed PPM

    Expected PPM is the share of a normal distribution beyond each limit, times one million: Φ((LSL − x̄) / σ) + 1 − Φ((USL − x̄) / σ), shown for both sigmas. Observed PPM counts the readings actually outside the limits.

    Normality check

    An Anderson-Darling test with the D'Agostino-Stephens small-sample adjustment, run when there are 8 or more readings. If p is below 0.05 the data may not be normal, and the indices and expected PPM should be read with caution.

    Verdict

    PPAP-style initial process study criteria, on Ppk by default: above 1.67 acceptable, 1.33 to 1.67 may be acceptable with customer approval, below 1.33 not acceptable. Your customer may set different limits; theirs apply.

    Assumptions

    The process should be stable (in statistical control) and the readings in production order, because the moving range and subgroups depend on order. Use 30 or more readings for a dependable estimate; with fewer, the result carries a warning.

    Quick reference

    Cp, Cpk, Pp and Ppk in one minute.

    What's the difference between Cp and Cpk?

    Cp compares the width of the tolerance with the spread of the process, ignoring where the process is centered. Cpk also accounts for centering: it measures the distance from the mean to the nearer spec limit. A process can have a high Cp and a low Cpk if it runs off-center.

    What's the difference between Cpk and Ppk?

    Both use the same formula. Cpk uses within (short-term) sigma, estimated from moving ranges or subgroup ranges. Ppk uses overall sigma, the standard deviation of all readings, so it includes shifts and drift over the study. Ppk is usually the one customers ask for in an initial PPAP study.

    What's a good Cpk?

    Many automotive customers require 1.33 or more for ongoing production and 1.67 or more for initial studies and special characteristics. Always check your customer's specific requirements.

    What if I only have one spec limit?

    Leave the other limit blank. You'll get the one-sided index (Ppu or Ppl, and Cpu or Cpl); Cp and Pp need both limits and aren't shown.

    Is my data stored anywhere?

    No. The calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript. The readings and limits you enter are never sent to Mensuro or anyone else, and this page doesn't save them.
