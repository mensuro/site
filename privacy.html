Privacy

Last updated October 2026. This page covers the Mensuro website, the early-access list and the private preview of the tool.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the early-access list.
  • Your optional answers about your role, current tools and most-used analysis.
  • The campaign tag of the link you followed to the site, if it had one (for example, from a LinkedIn post), saved with your signup so we know which posts bring people here.
  • Visit counts: which pages are viewed, the referring site, and the country and type of device, measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and doesn't identify you.

What we don't collect

  • This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts. Its only third-party script is Cloudflare Web Analytics, for the visit counts above.
  • The free Cpk calculator runs entirely in your browser. The readings and limits you enter are never sent to us or stored.
  • Your email and that campaign tag are kept in your browser tab only until you sign up, and are cleared when you close the tab.

Why we collect it

To tell you when your early-access spot opens, to decide which analyses to build first, and to understand how people find the site. We do not sell or rent your information.

Who processes it

  • Netlify, which hosts this site and stores your email address and optional answers.
  • Cloudflare, which counts site visits (Web Analytics).
  • Google Cloud (United States), which runs the Mensuro server that analyses files in the private preview.
  • Stripe, which processes payment if you choose the founding membership. We never see your card details.

Your choices

We only email you about Mensuro: your early-access spot, launch news and the founding offer. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. To see or delete what we hold about you, email nick@mensuro.tech and we will act on it within 30 days.

Product data

In the private preview, a file you upload goes to the Mensuro server over an encrypted connection, is analysed in memory and is discarded as soon as the results come back. It is never saved, logged or shared. Report details you type (part number, company, logo) are used only to build that PDF; your company, customer, name, paper size and logo are remembered in your own browser so you don't retype them. A full product privacy policy will be published before beta opens.
