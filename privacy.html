Privacy

Last updated October 2026. This page covers the Mensuro website and early-access list.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the early-access list.
  • Your optional answers about your role, current tools and most-used analysis.
  • The campaign tag of the link you followed to the site, if it had one (for example, from a LinkedIn post), saved with your signup so we know which posts bring people here.
  • Basic visit statistics such as page views and referring sites, if we turn them on. Any tool we use for this will be cookie-free.

What we don't collect

  • This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The free Cpk calculator runs entirely in your browser. The readings and limits you enter are never sent to us or stored.
  • Your email and that campaign tag are kept in your browser tab only until you sign up, and are cleared when you close the tab.

Why we collect it

To tell you when your early-access spot opens, to decide which analyses to build first, and to understand how people find the site. We do not sell or rent your information.

Who processes it

  • Netlify, which hosts this site and stores your email address and optional answers.
  • Stripe, which processes payment if you choose the founding membership. We never see your card details.

Your choices

We only email you about Mensuro: your early-access spot, launch news and the founding offer. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. To see or delete what we hold about you, email hello@mensuro.tech and we will act on it within 30 days.

Product data

When the product launches, measurement data you upload will be used only to compute your report and deleted afterward unless you save the study. A full product privacy policy will be published before beta opens.
